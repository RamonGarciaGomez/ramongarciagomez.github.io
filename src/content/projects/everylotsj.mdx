Every 2 minutes, a bot picks a random San José address from a database of 394,000,
fetches a Google Street View photo, and posts it to Mastodon. No curation — just
the city as it actually is, one address at a time.

→ [Live dashboard](/everylotsj) — progress map, current address, estimated completion  
→ [Follow on Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/@everylotsj)

## Stack

- **Language**: Python
- **Data**: City of San José open GIS data (394,000 address points)
- **Images**: Google Street View Static API
- **Posting**: Mastodon.py
- **Storage**: SQLite
- **Infrastructure**: Google Cloud e2-micro VM, cron every 2 minutes
